Ship models used by layers/ais.js.

Source: Kenney's Watercraft Kit (https://kenney.nl/assets/watercraft-kit),
via the glTF-converted mirror at https://github.com/shorepine/kenney
(3d/watercraft/). Only a small subset of the full 46-model kit is used
here — see MODEL_FOR_TYPE in ais.js for which file maps to which AIS
ship type.
