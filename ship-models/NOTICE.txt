Ship/buoy models used by layers/ais.js (ships, by AIS type — see
modelForType()) and layers/ports.js (buoy.glb, for NOWPHAS wave buoys).

Source: Kenney's Watercraft Kit (https://kenney.nl/assets/watercraft-kit),
via the glTF-converted mirror at https://github.com/shorepine/kenney
(3d/watercraft/). Only a small subset of the full 46-model kit is used
here.

Textures/colormap.png is a single shared texture atlas used by every
model in the kit — each .glb references it by the relative URI
"Textures/colormap.png", which is why it has to sit in that same
subfolder here rather than next to the .glb files directly.
